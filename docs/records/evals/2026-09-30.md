## The js fixture was not the committed fixture

Every js run, in both arms, audited a fixture the scaffold had changed. The
scaffold copied `examples/js-payments-service/{broken,clean}/` whole,
including the `node_modules/` git ignores, then deleted every line naming a
violation from every file. ESLint's own source has such lines
(`lib/services/suppressions-service.js`), so the staged ESLint crashed on
load with a `SyntaxError`. Five of the six recoverable js-clean reports
mention it: the plugin arm's run 1 filed it `advisory` and run 2
`needs-review`; run 3 does not mention it, and says the layering rules are
met "and a lint rule enforces them", which the staged fixture did not bear
out; the no-plugin arm rated it High (runs 1, 3) or Info (run 2). The
js-broken reports were staged the same way. The py fixtures were staged
with an untracked `.import_linter_cache` too; it appears in the coverage
maps of all three plugin-arm py-clean reports, enlarging their
denominators, and no finding cites it.

The scaffolds now stage the fixture's files at the commit (`git archive`),
refuse to stage nothing, and `scripts/test_eval_scaffolds.py` holds them to
it (this record's PR).
